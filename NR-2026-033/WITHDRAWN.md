# NR-2026-033 - withdrawn 2026-08-29

This advisory has been withdrawn from publication at the vendor's request.

The finding concerns rippled (XRP Ledger). Ripple advised us that mitigations are not yet uniformly
deployed across the independently operated nodes on the public network, and that the residual risk is
carried by third-party operators rather than by Ripple. We agree that is a good reason to wait, so the
write-up is offline until the fix is deployed widely enough that publishing it does not expose
operators who have not yet updated.

No technical detail is retained on this page by design.

We intend to republish once that condition is met. If you are a node operator who needs the detail in
order to assess or mitigate your own exposure, contact simon@nullrabbit.ai.
